# Style catalogue

Pick one entry from each list, avoiding everything in `video/ads/LEDGER.md`. Combinations that clash on purpose (a heist film about splitting chiya) work best. Every option is achievable with keyframes → Veo, or with Remotion alone.

## Genre / format
1. Deadpan funeral or eulogy melodrama (Thai-insurance-ad tradition): **used in film 1**
2. Nature documentary: a hushed narrator observes "the Kathmandu diner in its natural habitat" as the bill approaches
3. True-crime docuseries: interviews, case files, "the night of the missing 840 rupees"
4. Heist film: a crew plans the perfect exit before the bill comes; slick, cool and absurd
5. Courtroom drama: "The People vs. Suresh", with exhibits (receipts) and a gavel
6. 90s NTV / Doordarshan-era TV commercial: VHS, jingle, cheesy family, played lovingly
7. Breaking news broadcast: anchor, ticker, live reporter outside a momo shop
8. Horror trailer: "THE BILL", with whispering, a door creak, a hand reaching for the folder
9. Sports commentary: cricket-style ball-by-ball as friends avoid the bill, with replays
10. Bollywood or Lok Dohori musical number: a sung argument (dohori is a sung back-and-forth; perfect for a debt dispute)
11. Spaghetti-western standoff over the last momo
12. Mockumentary interviews (The Office style), with talking heads and awkward zooms
13. Silent film: black and white, intertitles, piano, slapstick
14. Wedding video with an unexpected speech
15. Space or mission-control launch countdown (Houston, we have a split)
16. Love-story montage across 40 years, told through bills
17. Self-help guru or infomercial parody ("But wait, there's more")
18. Single unbroken take through a restaurant (oner)
19. Kid's school-essay voiceover: "My dad's friend Rajan" (honest child narrator)
20. Letter or voice-note read aloud by a grandparent

## Visual medium
- Photoreal live action (keyframes → Veo), 35 mm grade: **film 1**
- Black and white, high contrast, 4:3 pillarboxed inside 9:16
- VHS / camcorder found footage, with date stamp, tracking lines and on-camera mic audio
- Claymation / stop-motion look (generated stills with clay textures, stepped at 12 fps)
- Traditional painting come alive: Mithila (Madhubani) or Paubha / Thangka linework, gently animated in Remotion
- Hand-drawn 2D sketchbook, pencil on lokta paper (frames generated, boiled lines)
- Security camera / CCTV multi-view grid
- Phone-screen POV: the whole film happens in a group chat, notifications and calls
- Miniature / tilt-shift world
- Archival newsreel with sepia and scratches
- Bold flat graphic poster style (Saul Bass), title-sequence energy

## Narrator / voice
Eulogist (first person, deadpan): **film 1** · documentary hush · gravel-voiced trailer man · over-excited cricket commentator · child · grandmother in Nepali with subtitles · no narration at all (pure picture and sound) · the object narrates (the momo plate, the bill folder, the 500-rupee note) · sung (dohori duet) · news anchor and field reporter

## Music
Sarangi + harmonium funeral score → madal jingle: **film 1** · Morricone whistle and guitar · 90s synth jingle with a sung brand name · tense heist bass and strings · solo piano ragtime (silent film) · horror drones and a music box · cricket-broadcast brass sting · Lok Dohori madal and voices · total silence plus Foley only · lo-fi beat

## Twist mechanism (never reuse one)
- **Reincarnation / the next life**: debt follows you, the catchphrase taken literally: **film 1**
- **Narrator reveal**: the story was told by someone (or something) unexpected (the bill itself, the waiter, the dog, a ghost)
- **Perspective flip**: the "villain" all along was the narrator, or the viewer ("this ad was paid for by Suresh, finally")
- **Time loop**: the end returns to the first frame, slightly changed
- **Literalised idiom**: a Nepali or English saying made physical ("paisa ko bot", "money doesn't grow on trees"; "khutta tanne")
- **Genre swerve**: two seconds of a completely different film that recontextualises everything
- **The scale reveal**: pull back and the drama was tiny (two ants splitting a crumb, a dollhouse)
- **Chekhov's object**: an object in the background of shot 1 is the payoff
- **Festival logic**: Tihar (Kukur, Kaag, Laxmi Puja), Dashain tika money, Teej; the custom explains the ending
- **Cosmic accounting**: Yamaraj or Chitragupta's ledger in the afterlife, already using the app

## Hook device (never reuse one)
Shock statement about death and money: **film 1** · a question no one asks out loud · an extreme close-up mystery · a loud crash or sound first · on-screen text that contradicts the picture · starting mid-argument · a countdown · a news "BREAKING" sting · a baby / animal doing something human · silence and one face
