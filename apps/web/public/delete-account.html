Delete your account & data

Split-Up, operated by Thimitech · Last updated 3 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Split-Up account and the data linked to it, and how to delete some of your data without closing your account.

Delete your entire account

Email ravi@thimitech.com from the email address on your Split-Up account, with the subject “Delete my Split-Up account”. We verify the request and process it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — name, email, phone number, and payment handles (eSewa/Khalti IDs, payment QR).
  • Your login sessions and friend code.
  • Expenses, groups and settle-ups that involve only you.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Shared financial records. Expenses or settle-ups you shared with other people remain part of their records so their balances stay correct. Your personal details are removed or anonymised from them.
  • Legal/accounting records. Where law requires, limited records may be retained for the mandated period, then deleted.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • In the app: delete individual expenses, remove a friend, or edit/clear your profile fields and payment handles at any time.
  • By request: email ravi@thimitech.com to have specific data removed.

Questions

Contact ravi@thimitech.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
