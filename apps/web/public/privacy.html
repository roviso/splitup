Split-Up

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Split-Up (“we”, “us”) is a bill-splitting service operated by Thimitech. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. It applies to the Split-Up website (splitup.thimitech.com) and the Split-Up Android and iOS apps.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, and the email address and/or phone number you sign up with. If you use “Continue with Google”, we receive your Google account’s email and name.
  • Payment handles you choose to add — your eSewa ID, Khalti ID, and a payment QR image, so friends can pay you back. We do not process payments or collect card, bank or wallet passwords.
  • Your bill-splitting data — groups, expenses, amounts, who paid, how a bill is split, settle-ups, notes and activity you enter in the app.
  • Contacts you choose to import — if you use the “import contacts” feature to find or invite friends, the names, phone numbers and emails of those contacts are sent to our server to match existing users and create invitations. Importing is optional and only happens when you start it.
  • Technical data — a login session token (stored on your device to keep you signed in) and basic server logs such as IP address, used to keep the service secure and rate-limited.

How we use it

  • To run the core features: create groups, split bills, track who owes whom, and sync changes live to the people you share with.
  • To let friends identify and repay you (your name and the payment handles you added are shown to people you split with).
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To send login/verification codes and the notifications you enable (e.g. WhatsApp reminders you trigger).

How information is shared

  • With people you split with. Your name, the expenses you add them to, balances, and any payment handles you set are visible to the friends and group members involved — that’s how splitting works.
  • Service providers. We use an email provider to send codes and Google’s sign-in service if you choose it. These process data only to provide that function.
  • We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

Data storage & security

Data is stored on our servers. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never in plain text. Login sessions use tokens, and traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. No online service can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data, except where we must keep limited records to comply with law. Expenses shared with others may remain visible to those others as part of their own records.

Your choices & rights

  • Access or update your profile and payment handles at any time in the app.
  • Skip contact import — it is never automatic.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us.

Children

Split-Up is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
