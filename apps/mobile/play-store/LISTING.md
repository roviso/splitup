## Full description (max 4000 chars)
```
Split-Up makes sharing bills with friends, flatmates and travel groups simple — built for how money actually works in Nepal.

Free and unlimited. No daily cap on expenses, no paywall on the basics.

FIVE WAYS TO SPLIT
• Equally between everyone
• Exact amounts per person
• Percentages
• Shares (e.g. 2 parts for you, 1 for a friend)
• Itemized bills — each person pays for what they actually ate, with 10% service charge and 13% VAT shared out proportionally

SETTLE UP THE NEPALI WAY
Pay back over eSewa, Khalti or Fonepay. Friends see your wallet ID and QR code right when they go to pay you. The receiver taps “Got it ✓” and the payer sees it confirmed — no more “did you get it?”.

SMARTER GROUPS
• Multiple payers on a single expense
• Debt simplification so a group settles up in the fewest possible payments
• Groups for trips, flats, dinners — with an activity feed and undo (restore a deleted expense)

ADD FRIENDS IN SECONDS
Everyone gets a personal friend code and QR. Scan a friend’s code with your camera or the in-app scanner, or look them up by code or email. Adding is mutual and instant. Import contacts to find people already on Split-Up, or invite anyone by WhatsApp or link — their shared history links to their account the moment they join.

LIVE SYNC
Groups, expenses and settle-ups appear on everyone’s screen as they happen, with pop-down banners and a “For you” inbox. Nudge people who owe you, right from the app.

MADE FOR NEPAL
• Full English and नेपाली — Devanagari numerals and lakh grouping
• Dates in Bikram Sambat (B.S.) or A.D.
• Rounding and money handled in paisa, so balances are always exact

EASY, PRIVATE SIGN-IN
Sign up with email + password, a passwordless email code, or Google. We never sell your data.

Split the bill, keep the friendship. Download Split-Up.
```
