# NEXT TIME — a SplitUp spot (9:16, ~45 s)

**Style:** deadpan funeral melodrama, played completely straight, in the tradition of Thai insurance ads. Warm 35 mm film, a solemn sarangi and harmonium score, and the comedy comes from the contrast. Classic TV-spot shape: story → product → logo → a post-logo tag.

**Logline:** A man gives his best friend's eulogy, and it turns out to be a list of what the friend owes him.

**The twist:** after the logo, a puppy with Ramesh's exact moustache is sitting on Suresh's doorstep. At first it seems unrelated. Then it lands: in Nepali belief, a debt (ऋण) not paid in this life follows you into the next. Ramesh always promised "next time". This *is* next time.

## Cast
- **Ramesh** (deceased, ~32): round, cheerful face; a thick black handlebar moustache (his signature); a Dhaka topi; a huge grin.
- **Suresh** (~32, narrator): thin, rectangular glasses, neat side parting, serious. Wears white (mourning).
- **The puppy:** a tan Nepali street puppy with a black marking above its mouth in the exact shape of Ramesh's handlebar moustache.
