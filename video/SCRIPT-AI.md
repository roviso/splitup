# Split-Up "Smart Split" film: script

**Film 2 of the series.** The launch film sold the app. This one sells the AI.
**Format:** 72 s at 30 fps, in two cuts from one timeline and one audio master: **16:9** (1920×1080) and **9:16** (1080×1920, for Reels, Shorts and TikTok). Everything is seen on a phone: the Android app is a Capacitor shell around splitup.thimitech.com, so every product shot is a real 412 px (Pixel 8) screen from the live app.
**Voice:** Google `gemini-3.8-flash-tts`, voice *Sulafat* (the same narrator as the launch film, so the series sounds like one brand). Recorded as ONE take and then cut into lines.
**Look:** same lokta-paper and marigold `#f2a007` system as the launch film. The new visual idea is **the receipt as the main object**: a thermal bill that curls, tears and turns into the app. The app's own scalloped "torn receipt" card edge becomes the transition between shots.

**About 200 words, 72 seconds as recorded.**

### Why this is different from the launch film
- **Structure:** the launch film was problem → diagnosis 01/02/03 → feature tour. This one is a **callback loop**: the same bill arrives twice, the first time as chaos and the second time as a two-second snap. The contrast is the pitch.
- **Main object:** the receipt, not kinetic type. It unrolls (the problem), gets scanned (the AI) and becomes the app's torn-edge share card (the answer).
- **Humour:** "one chowmein nobody remembers ordering". That's a real table moment, and it sets up the AI line where Sagar gets the chowmein.
- **Growth loop in the story:** the referral isn't tacked on at the end. The invite line is also the "stay friends" idea, because you share the app with the same gang.

## Director's notes for the voice
The same warm narrator as the launch film. The opening is fond and lively ("the whole gang" with a smile), then the list in line 2 speeds up and gets more exasperated until it lands flat on "the calculator". "Put the calculator away" is quiet and confident, with a small pause before it. The product section is calm, clear and unhurried: let "to the paisa" land. The referral lines are playful, as if you're letting the viewer in on a trick. In the payoff, slow down on "before the tea gets cold". The CTA is warm, with three even beats.

## Things to fix or avoid before the shoot (found while testing)
- **The Romanized Nepali reply mixes scripts.** It came back as "Momo ko bill draft gareँ … maile tireko राखेँ।", with a Devanagari chandrabindu on Latin text. It happened again in the final capture ("gareँ", "tapaiँ"). In shot 9 the user's typed message bubble sits over that reply line. The real fix is in the chat prompt in `apps/api/src/ai.ts`: for Romanized input, reply fully in Roman script.
- **The bill total wraps on a phone.** "Rs / 3,455.54" breaks onto two lines in the Smart Split header at 390 px. The film's screens are captured at 412 px (Pixel 8 width), where it fits on one line.
- **"1 friends joined with your code"** needs a plural rule.
- **AI credits spent on this account:** 4 (15 → 11). Research used one scan and one chat, and the film capture another scan and chat. Nothing was saved.

## Final cut (measured)

One Gemini TTS take, cut at real pauses. Every line is leveled to −18 LUFS, and the dead air between lines is ≤ 0.085 s (checked by `scripts/check-voice.mjs`: PASS). The music is an original Lyria 3.5 bed, ducked under the voice with a sidechain. The master is −16.0 LUFS integrated with a −1.5 dBTP true peak.
